# Twinly Video Design

## Overview
Twinly is a dark, landscape-first baby care app for twins. The visual mood is calm, practical, and trustworthy, with big controls and a clear A/B split so caregivers can act fast during busy moments.

## Brand Feel
- Warm but not cute
- Confident, tidy, and a little premium
- Built for speed, one-handed use, and low stress

## Color Palette
- Background: `#081120`
- Panel: `#101C33`
- Surface: `#172544`
- Milk blue: `#3B82F6`
- Diaper amber: `#F59E0B`
- Accent mint: `#34D399`
- Text primary: `#F8FAFC`
- Text secondary: `#94A3B8`

## Typography
- Headings: system sans, bold, tight tracking
- Body: system sans, medium weight
- Numbers: tabular, large, highly legible

## Visual Components
- Two side-by-side baby panels
- Large milk and diaper buttons
- Small status chips for sync and PWA
- Log cards with timestamps
- Bottom snackbar for undo

## Motion Language
- Use soft slide-ins and gentle scale pops
- Keep button pulses readable and reassuring
- Use one strong title reveal and one strong CTA reveal

## Do
- Show A/B simultaneously
- Keep text large and sparse
- Let the buttons feel touchable and fast

## Don't
- Avoid busy UI chrome
- Avoid playful pastel visuals
- Avoid thin type or tiny labels
